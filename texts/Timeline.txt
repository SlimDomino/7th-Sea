THÉAH TIMELINE
A history of Théah as it is known in 1669 AV
Blood & Iron campaign reference (player edition)
================================================================

HOW TO READ THIS
- This is the history of Théah as a well-read Théan would know it: public record, church history, and common legend. Where scholars disagree, the commonly told version is given.
- Calendar: AUC counts years from the founding of Numa. AV (Anno Veritas) counts from the First Prophet, so 774 AUC = 1 AV. The year has twelve 30-day months, Primus through Decimus, followed by the Prophets' Mass.
- The Blood & Iron campaign begins in Quintus 1669 AV. The situation described under "1668 AV" below still holds at that point: l'Empereur rules Montaigne, Montaigne still occupies western Castille, and the Vaticine Church has no Hierophant.


I. BEFORE HUMANITY
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Long before humanity, Théah belonged to the Syrneth, the "Old Races." Only ruins, strange artifacts, and bones remain, and the Explorer's Society exists to study them.
- The drachen of Eisen were Syrneth giants. Their bones lie beneath Eisen's soil, and the few drachen left today are small, degenerate beasts.
- Vesten legend: Grumfather made the world from the body of the giant Värld. When the Great Wyrm attacked, 25 warriors gave their lives and became the Living Runes.


II. THE OLD REPUBLIC (AUC)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- 1–340 AUC: Numa is founded, and the Old Republic rises.
- 236–255 AUC: Numan legions campaign against the Eisen tribes and are driven out.
- 344 AUC onward: Senator Caius Castillus leads Numa's conquest of Acraga (later Castille).
- 502–508 AUC: Tribal raids destroy the Republic's colonies north of the River.
- 698 AUC: General Gaius Philippus Macer seizes power from the Senate. The Republic gives way to the Empire, and the Senators somehow acquire sorcery.


III. 1 AV – 999 AV
----------------------------------------------------------------
- 1 AV (774 AUC): The First Prophet speaks on the Senate floor and condemns sorcery. He is executed, and he foretells that three more Prophets will come.
- 32 AV: The Crescent Empire expels Numa from its lands.
- 98 AV: Legio II halts an Ussuran invasion.
- 100–300 AV: The Empire splits into East and West.
- 105 AV: The Eastern Imperator recognizes the Church.
- c. 200 AV: The Imperator converts to the Prophet's faith, and the sorcerous Senators flee to the provinces.
- 297 AV: Eisen tribes (the Brutomanni) sack Numa. The Empire collapses and the Dark Ages begin.
- 297–312 AV: The Castillo family become the first kings of Castille.
- 305–306 AV: The Second Prophet comes from the East and is killed in Crescent lands. His death starts the first crusades.
- 305–1000 AV: Castille is long allied with, and then occupied by, the Crescent Empire. San Cristóbal is founded as Hil'al.
- 306 AV: Jaala Khan unites the Kosar.
- 312–376 AV: Imperator Corantine orders a single Credo, creating the Vaticine Church with a Hierophant at Numa. The Ussuran Orthodox Church splits away over the Credo.
- c. 400 AV: The first White Plague.
- c. 430 AV: The Prophets' message reaches Inismore.
- 523–525 AV: Johann von der Velde invades Ussura. In 525 the first Gaius unites the five Ussuran kingdoms.
- c. 600 AV: Imperator Carleman unites the northern lands and divides them among his sons: Stefan takes Eisen, Charles the west, and Iago the east.
- 7th century: Charles weds Isabeau Montanus, who founds Montaigne and later marries Léon "the Weak."
- 686 AV: Dracheneisen is discovered, and Stefan creates the first barons (Pösen, Heilgrund).
- 691 AV: Stefan names his kingdom Eisen.
- 782 AV: Gottschalk I conquers Vodacce and is crowned the first Imperator of "the Holy Republic."
- 818 AV: Gunnef the Ravenhaired unites the Vestenmannavnjar and becomes the first High King.
- 820–1020 AV: The age of Vesten raiding.
- 900–1500 AV: The age of the Vesten High Kings.
- 914 AV: The White Plague returns and kills more than a third of Théah, worst in Montaigne, Vodacce and the Crescent Empire. It lingers on and off for about four centuries.


IV. 1000 AV – 1499 AV
----------------------------------------------------------------
- c. 1000 AV: The Third Prophet appears in northern Castille.
- 1002–1011: A bishop is murdered at Malaca, starting the Second Crusades. In 1009 High King Garcia's Crescent armies are defeated at Malaca. Castille's sorcerous nobles are purified in El Fuego Sagrado, and the survivors flee to Rancho Gallegos.
- 1011: Vinchenzo nearly unites Vodacce.
- 1012–1019: The Hierophant (Hieros) War. The Church's capital moves to Castille and the Inquisition is founded. Mad Queen Marietta Lorenzo dies in 1012.
- 1014: Ramón Sandoval is crowned High King of Castille. The O'Bannon legend begins in Inismore.
- 1028: Montaigne conquers Avalon, Inismore and the Highland Marches, and the Sidhe withdraw.
- 1030: The Third Prophet dies.
- 1088: The Vodacce cardinals demand the libraries of Alexia.
- c. 1100–1312: The crusader era.
- 1118: The Poor Knights of the Prophet are founded.
- 12th century: The Altamira riverport is established.
- 1133: The first bullfight, at Tarago.
- 1153–1216: Avalon lives under Montaigne rule. The outlaw Robin Goodfellow is active from 1157 to 1199. The Council of Free Nobles forms in 1215, and the rightful king returns in 1216.
- 1175: The Lorenzo line of Vodacce dies out.
- 1218–1219: The first King of the Highland Marches.
- 1267: Imperator Stefan builds the fortress called the Stein around the Wachtturm, a tower of drachen bone. Freiburg will later grow up on the site.
- 1307–1312: The Poor Knights are declared heretics and destroyed. Their Grand Master, Jacques du Muguet, is burned at the stake.
- 1312–1355: Justice Rogers, the first famous pirate.
- 1347: A White Plague outbreak. By some accounts nearly a third of Théah dies.
- 1369–1412: Life of Scarovese, who writes the treatises on the Vodacce "Great Game."
- 1367 or 1376: The Heart of the Drachen Cathedral is built from a drachen skeleton.
- 1386: The White Plague kills Castille's entire royal family in San Cristóbal.
- 1387–1422: Avalon's kings David III and David IV briefly hold western Montaigne.
- 1398–1400: The Bianco family of Vodacce is exposed as Legion-worshippers and destroyed by the Vestini.
- 1402: The explorer Cristóbal Gallegos sails west and is never seen again.
- 1411: The Kreuzritter (the Black Knights) are wiped out at Tannen.
- 1438: Tobias Vogel invents the printing press in Pösen.
- 1450–1650: Two centuries of on-and-off war between Avalon and Montaigne.
- 1478: Albert Tuttenburg's cannon sinks a pirate raider off the Stein.
- 1479: The Assumption Cathedral in Sousdal is completed.


V. 1500 AV – 1635 AV
----------------------------------------------------------------
- 1501: Famine in Grimstadd. Inger Holmstrom's carls go abroad to trade, beginning "the Rise of Vendel."
- 1516: Holmstrom's merchant council forms with the backing of Imperator Franz II.
- 1517: Matthias Lieber posts his questions in October. Franz II's knights rescue him, and Lieber hides from the Hierophant's men beneath a smithy at the Stein. His book spreads Objectionism to a third of the faithful within a decade. Castille bans it.
- 1528: The Vesten nation is renamed Vendel.
- 1534–c. 1544: Avalon annexes Inismore.
- 1535: The Graal is published, and "Richard the Kind" embraces it.
- 1547: Ulf Hövsgaard becomes High King of the Vesten.
- 1552–1571: Cardinal Orduñez builds a model prison on la Palabra de Dios. It becomes La Bucca.
- 1563: The Vaticine Church calls for pious baroque art.
- 1565: The last Vesten High King vanishes after a failed raid on Montaigne.
- 1587: Weiss III ends the persecution of Objectionists in Eisen. In June, Cameron MacCormick finds amber-encased Syrneth "armor" south of Vodacce.
- 1589: MacCormick breaks with Allario Caligari and his "diggers."
- 1592: Burke University is founded in Avalon.
- 1593: François Valroux du Martise founds the Valroux fencing school.
- 1598: The Explorer's Society is founded in Carleon. Chapter houses follow at Tannen (1599), Kirk (1600) and Altamira (1603, under Alejandro Diega).
- 1600: Prisoners revolt at La Bucca. By now Vendel's league of guilds dominates trade in the north.
- 1605: Breutzmann's School for Young Nobles is founded.
- 1608: The microscope is invented.
- 1610: The anatomical map is published, and the Scientific Renaissance begins.
- 1612: The Drachen Wing inn is built.
- 1613: Castille develops its quarantine method. The Knights of the Rose and Cross announce themselves.
- 1614: Richard IV takes the throne of Avalon.
- 1620: Jeremy Cook founds the Royal Fraternity for Scientific Minds.
- c. 1620–1666: Vendel and Vodacce fight an undeclared trade war.
- 1622: Richard IV's Act of Supremacy creates the Church of Avalon. His daughter Elaine is born to Morwenna Sutter. Violent storms ravage northeast Vodacce.
- 1627: Alcide Mondavi and Ferenc von Durenstadt are born. Darius Olaf disappears.


VI. 1636 AV – 1667 AV: THE WAR ERA
----------------------------------------------------------------
- 1636: The War of the Cross begins. Imperator Weiss dies and the Vaticine Reifenstahl succeeds him. Objectionist governors are thrown from a window, and Stauss crushes the revolt that follows.
- 1637: The Vendel Objectionist Stefano Wulf invades Eisen with a "volunteer army." He takes the Stein in its last battle that winter. The fortress is abandoned in 1639.
- The war grinds on under Dehmer and then Gietl. The southern barons secede, and Wulf is eventually killed by a bauer.
- 1639–1664: The ruined Stein is rebuilt as a trade city.
- 1642: Valentina Vestini (later Villanova) is born.
- c. 1644: Princess Elaine of Avalon vanishes on her wedding night.
- 1647: Léon XIV of Montaigne marries the Fate Witch Morella Caligari.
- 1649: Ilya is born in Ussura. Faulk Fischler finds dracheneisen.
- 1652: A fire burns the Castillian university library.
- Spring equinox 1654: Margaret, Avalon's "Iron Queen," dies.
- 1656: Elaine returns with the Graal, and Glamour returns to Avalon. The Freiburg Gazette, Théah's first newspaper, begins printing. Vincent Bernvadore becomes Headmaster of the Explorer's Society.
- 1657: Ravenild Hibbot joins the Royal Fraternity.
- 1658: Elaine unites Avalon, Inismore and the Highland Marches under one crown. In Ussura, Gaius Nikolai and his wife die, and nine-year-old Ilya becomes Gaius; his hair turns white overnight.
- 1659: The Castillian Armada is destroyed: 180 ships and 36,000 men lost under Admiral Hernando Arciniega de Orduño. The Orduños are disgraced, and Prince Javier is made Admiral.
- 1661: Jeremy Cook publishes The Rational Chemist. Franz Deleboe is assassinated. Oskar Steil's academy is founded.
- 1662: Jeremy Cook is killed by the Inquisition.
- c. 1663–1664: Castille's King falls ill and Prince Javier rules as regent. Then Javier vanishes, and the King dies.
- 1664:
  - Léon XIV declares himself a sorcerer and offers sanctuary to sorcerers.
  - The Château du Soleil is finished.
  - Eisenfürst Trägue names the rebuilt city Freiburg.
  - Vendel launches its currency, the guilder.
  - The War of the Cross winds down.
  - The Explorer's Society's first voyage in search of "new lands" sails west. None of its ships has returned.
- Spring 1665: Thirteen-year-old Salvador Bejarano de Sandoval is crowned King of Castille, but without the title Rex Castillium.
- 1665: El Vago makes his first appearance, rescuing Lucina Garcia on Rancho Aldana.
- Nonus 1665 – Primus 1666: The White Plague returns to Avalon, Castille and Montaigne.
- 1666:
  - The Hierophant dies after a visit to Montaigne, officially of plague.
  - Verdugo's Castillian army attacks Charouse and is turned back at Montegue's Stand. Léon proclaims himself Empereur and orders the invasion of Castille.
  - The Treaty of Weissberg ends the War of the Cross and partitions Eisen. Castille and Montaigne each take a share.
  - Septimus 17: The prisoners of La Bucca revolt, using a faked White Plague case. They found the Brotherhood of the Coast.
  - Summer: The scientist Alvara Arciniega goes into hiding. Etienne Deneuve opens the Explorer chapter house in Charouse.
- 1667:
  - Spring: Don Marco Ontiveros del Ochoa betrays Barcino. General Montegue overruns Rancho Torres, whose ruling family loses everything, and most of Zepeda. The San Juan massacre. La Reina del Mar falls. Montegue crosses La Selva de Fendes and destroys the forts north of El Morro.
  - Montegue is recalled and ordered to invade Ussura. Without him, the Montaigne assault on El Morro fails, and a Castillian counteroffensive retakes the east bank in seven days.
  - Late 1667: The wall called La Muralla al Ultimo holds. The front has been in stalemate since the fall.
  - Imperator Reifenstahl hangs himself after a visit from a cloaked stranger. The barons become the seven Eisenfürsten.
  - Spring: A toxic Crescent "bladder-ring" kills 17 Explorers.


VII. 1668 AV – 1669 AV: THE PRESENT DAY
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Castille: Between a quarter and a third of the country is occupied by Montaigne. There is no Hierophant, and Cardinal Verdugo's Inquisition rules in the young King's name. El Vago protects King Sandoval, now 16.
- Montaigne's advance has stalled while Montegue campaigns in Ussura.
- Alvara Arciniega builds the first reflecting telescope.
- Tertius 17, 1668: Bernvadore reports to the Explorer's Society conference in Carleon.
- Quintus 1669: The Blood & Iron campaign begins.
